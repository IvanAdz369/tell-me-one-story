2. What the app does

The app asks you one question a day and uses an AI narrator, Claude by Anthropic, to write your answers as pages and chapters of a book about your life. The pages are written by AI from what you tell it.

  • The writing can be wrong. It can misunderstand you or get a detail wrong. You can correct any page, and your correction always wins.
  • It is a personal keepsake, not a record of fact. Don't rely on it as evidence or as a factual account.
  • It is not therapy or professional advice. The "Threads" at the end of a chapter are observations, not advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or to a crisis line. In the Philippines, call the NCMH Crisis Hotline at 1553.

6. Keeping your book safe

If your phone's Android backup is on, Android may also copy it to your Google account, but Android stops backing up an app once its data passes 25 MB, which a book with photos can reach, so do not rely on the backup. We keep no copy, apart from any page or chapter you report. If you lose your phone or delete the app without saving an archive, we cannot recover your book. Use Save the complete archive regularly and keep the file somewhere safe.
