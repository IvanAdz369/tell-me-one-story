Delete your data

Tell Me One Story · by Francis Ivan Marie Adelan, who publishes on Google Play as Ivan AD

Delete your book in the app

Your book lives on your phone. There is no account to delete. To erase it:

  • Open Tell Me One Story and tap Keep.
  • Tap Delete everything, then Yes, erase my book.

This erases your name and other details, your stories, pages, chapters, photos, story bible, reminder, any review code, and the app's cache folder. It cannot be undone, so save the complete archive first if you might want your book back. Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored on your phone.

Copies the app cannot reach

  • Your phone's backup. If Android backup is on, a copy may be in your Google account. Turn backup off, or delete the backup, in your phone's settings or your Google account.
  • Files you saved or shared, such as a PDF or an archive. Delete them where you keep them.
  • Pages or chapters you reported. We keep them until they are reviewed, and for about 12 months at most.
  • Copies held by Anthropic, which writes the pages: normally deleted within 30 days, or kept up to 2 years if its safety systems flag them.
  • Subscription records at RevenueCat. Cancel the subscription itself in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions.
  • Our service's logs, which hold IP addresses and none of your words, are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Ask us to delete the rest

Write to francisivan.adelan@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". To find a report, include a few words of the page you reported. For subscription records, include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA). We delete what we hold within 30 days and reply to confirm.
